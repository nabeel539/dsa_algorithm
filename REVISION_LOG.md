# Spaced Repetition & Revision Log

This log tracks regular review sessions to solidify pattern intuition, eliminate bugs, and prepare for interviews through spaced repetition.

## Revision Schedule Guidelines
- **1st Review:** Within 24–48 hours after first solving. Focus on reproducing the algorithm without looking at the code.
- **2nd Review:** After 3–7 days. Focus on explaining the logic verbally, tracing edge cases, and writing clean Python code.
- **3rd Review:** After 2–4 weeks. Focus on speed, solving from a blank file under timed conditions (15–20 minutes).
- **Final Mock Review:** Prior to upcoming interview rounds.
